03 / BEFORE YOU DOWNLOAD

A few good
questions.

Which Wystone app is right for me?

Use Cality to see your calendar and Apple Reminders together, Stickly to block distracting apps and build habits, or MarkSave to save articles, videos and links for later. Each app works independently, so start with the one that solves your everyday problem.

Can Cality show my calendar and Apple Reminders together?

Yes. Cality connects to the calendars available through Apple’s calendar system and to Apple Reminders, with your permission. It is available for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Core features are free, with optional Pro features available in the app.

What does the Stickly app blocker do?

Stickly helps you block distracting apps on iPhone, set aside focused sessions and build habits. Explore the Stickly page for a closer look at its focus tools and the App Store for current availability and pricing.

Can I save articles and videos in MarkSave?

Yes. MarkSave keeps links, articles and videos in a private read later library. Read articles or watch videos in the app, and sync your library over iCloud.

How do I get help with an app?

Email support@wystoneapps.com or use the contact form below. Tell me which app and device you are using, and what happened. Your message goes to the person building the apps.
